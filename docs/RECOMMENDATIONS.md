# Executive Recommendations

1. Prioritise high-margin services and cloud products in regions where revenue is growing but target variance is negative.
2. Review accounts with strong revenue but weak margin for discount leakage and support cost pressure.
3. Use segment-level drill-through to separate enterprise growth from SMB volume effects.
4. Set monthly target reviews by region rather than quarterly-only reviews to surface underperformance earlier.
5. Expand partner enablement where marketplace revenue is growing but direct sales capacity is constrained.

Screenshot checklist for final Power BI submission:

- Executive overview with KPI cards and target variance.
- Monthly revenue and gross profit trend.
- Region/category/segment drill-down visual.
- Customer drill-through page.
- RLS role test screen showing restricted regional access.
